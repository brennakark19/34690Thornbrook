The path forward

Commit in stages. Resolve the biggest unknowns first.

01
Before an offer is binding

Confirm measured area above and below grade. Inspect roof, foundation, moisture, furnace, plumbing and sewer or septic. Get local agent feedback on resale range.

02
Before renovation spending

Written quotes for flooring, kitchen, baths and the deck. Structural review for the kitchen opening and any window or wall changes. Lock a scope that fits the budget.

03
First year

Essential repairs first, then flooring, kitchen, baths and finishes. Keep permits, receipts and before/after records.

04
At resale

Refresh comps, recalculate selling costs, then set the list price.

Verify before committing

What we still need to confirm.

  • Size: the listing shows 4,314 sq ft and describes a finished lower level. Confirm above-grade versus below-grade area before pricing, since the comps may count area differently.
  • Kitchen: removing the peninsula and adding a separate island needs measured planning and a structural review.
  • Basement: wood flooring below grade needs a moisture review. Missing ceiling tiles are replaced in the concept only; confirm condition and keep electrical and mechanical access clear.
  • Windows, walls and mechanicals: any alterations shown in the concepts need professional evaluation.
  • Draft numbers: offer, renovation budget and resale price are placeholders. Replace them before relying on the profit figure.
  • Margin is thin at the working case: about $30K at $550K resale and under $3K at $520K. Cost control is the project.
